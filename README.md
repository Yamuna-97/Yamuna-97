theory behind it. Most of my time goes into AI and computer vision — training
models, wiring up detection pipelines, and figuring out where they break in the
real world. The rest goes into full-stack builds, from React dashboards to
Node/Express backends. I've got a growing interest in cybersecurity and ethical
hacking, and I like projects that force me to touch every layer of the stack —
model, backend, and interface.

## Projects

### AI-Based Bird Protection System for Electrical Transmission Lines
> Computer-vision pipeline that detects birds near power lines and flags proximity risk.

**Tech:** `Python` `PyTorch` `YOLO` `OpenCV` `Computer Vision`

- Combines a trained bird-detection model with a YOLO11-based wire/power-line detector
- Uses monocular depth estimation to assess how close a detected bird is to live lines
- Status: actively in development — currently refining the wire-detection model

### Scripta
> A social media management platform with an analytics dashboard and AI-assisted content tools.

**Tech:** `MongoDB` `Express` `React` `Node.js` `Recharts`

- MERN-stack app with a React analytics dashboard (KPI cards, engagement metrics, sparklines)
- Includes an AI Studio module for batch campaign generation and media previews
- Status: in active development

### Relief-Link
> An AI-powered disaster response and resource coordination platform, built for an ideathon.

**Tech:** `AI` `Full-Stack` `System Design`

- Designed to coordinate resources and response during disaster scenarios
- Presented with system flowcharts and a competitor/feature comparison
